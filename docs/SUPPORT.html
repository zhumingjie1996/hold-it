常见问题

数据会上传到服务器吗？

不会。所有数据均存储在您的设备本地，我们不会收集任何信息。

如何恢复已购买的会员？

打开设置页面，点击「恢复购买」按钮即可。

数据如何备份？

如已开启 iCloud 备份，数据会随系统备份自动保存到您的 iCloud 个人空间。

FAQ

Is my data uploaded to a server?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect any information.

How do I restore my purchase?

Go to the Settings page and tap the "Restore Purchase" button.

How is my data backed up?

If you have iCloud Backup enabled, your data will be included automatically in your personal iCloud backup.
